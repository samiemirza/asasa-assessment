Build a gold trading experience people trust.
Turn live market data into a polished buy-and-sell experience.
01
The brief
Build a deployable, single-user demo for buying and selling gold using live market data. A
reviewer should be able to understand the starting balances, complete a trade, and see the
updated balances without extra guidance.
Product state
Seed a PKR wallet, customer gold holdings, and platform inventory.
Keep all three balances internally consistent after every trade.
Required trading flow
Show a live 24K rate in PKR per gram. Let the user enter PKR or gold,
then issue a 75-second locked quote for review and confirmation.
Finish with updated balances and a receipt.
Trust requirements
Show source and data freshness. Handle unavailable pricing,
insufficient cash, gold or inventory, expired quotes, and repeated
confirmation safely.
Out of scope
No authentication, real money movement, or admin panel. Mock or
seed anything else needed to keep the reviewer focused on the
trading experience.
Prioritize a coherent, finished feature set over gold plating (pun intended).
02
Pricing logic
Fetch pricing on the server no more than once every five minutes. Show the selected source and when it was
last refreshed. Pause trading if neither source can be trusted.
